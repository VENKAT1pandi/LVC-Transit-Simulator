# ADR-0005: Funding gap for West Seattle Link is an uncertain range, not a number

Status: accepted, 15 Nov 2026. Owner: project analyst; replace with the agency's figure when available.

## Decision

The materiality denominator is the West Seattle Link funding gap. It is drawn uniformly between $0.8 billion and $3.8 billion: the reported current cost estimate ($5.0–8.0 billion) minus the $4.2 billion finance-plan figure. The borrowing rate is drawn uniformly between 3.5% and 5.0%.

## Why

No Sound Transit document stating a project-level funding gap was found. Press reports disagree on dollar year and on whether the 2026 savings measures are included (see `data/reference/project_finance.csv`). The agency-wide gap of about $34.5 billion is context, not the denominator: a West Seattle instrument cannot be judged against the whole system's shortfall.

## Consequences

The funding gap is one of the largest drivers in the tornado chart. A finance-team figure would narrow the result more than any further econometric work. This is the spec's "collect more data" case.
